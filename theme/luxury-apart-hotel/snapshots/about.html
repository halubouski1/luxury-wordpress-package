BATUMI / ORBI CITY

A life by the water.

Batumi — Orbi City: modern seafront apartments just 50 meters from the sea. Nearby you’ll find the boulevard, gardens, restaurants, a children’s amusement area, and a dancing fountain lake, along with 24/7 convenience stores. The complex offers 24/7 security, and concierge service for guests.

Discover Batumi

NEW GUDAURI / FOUR SEASONS

A different perspective.

New Gudauri — Four Seasons: mountain-view apartments just 100 meters from the cable car and ski lifts. Nearby there’s an ice-skating rink for both adults and children, ski & snowboard rental shops (skis, snowboards, helmets, goggles, and all necessary gear), plus 24/7 shops and restaurants. Both surface and underground parking are available, along with 24/7 security and concierge service — ideal for winter vacations and investment.

Discover Gudauri
